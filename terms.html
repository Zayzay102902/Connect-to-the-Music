3. What the app does to your playlists

When you use a feature, the app makes real changes to your YouTube and Spotify accounts on your behalf, including:

  • creating playlists and adding or removing songs;
  • Sync Playlist, which replaces all songs in one platform's copy of a playlist with the songs from the other platform;
  • Keep Synced, which automatically copies songs you add or remove on one platform to the other, every few minutes, until you turn it off or delete the playlist;
  • Delete Playlist, which deletes the playlist on YouTube and/or unfollows it on Spotify.

Songs are matched between platforms by searching for the title and artist, so a match may sometimes be a different version of a song, or a song may not be found. Please review your playlists, and keep your own copy of any playlist you can't afford to lose.
