Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Connect to the Music ("the app," "we," "us") is a web app at connecttothemusic.app that lets you create, copy, sync and manage playlists across YouTube and Spotify, and build new playlists from a text description using AI. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used and stored, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

1. Data we access from Google (YouTube)

When you click "Continue with Google," you are asked to grant the app:

  • Basic Google profile sign-in. We receive your Google account ID so we can create and identify your account. We store only this ID; we do not store your name or email address.
  • Permission to view and manage your YouTube playlists. The app uses this to list your playlists and their videos, create new playlists, add and remove videos, and delete playlists, but only when you ask it to or when you turn on "Keep Synced" for a playlist.

2. Data we access from Spotify

  • Permission to view and manage your Spotify playlists. The app uses this to list your playlists and tracks, create playlists, add and remove tracks, and unfollow (delete) playlists, for the same features described above.

3. Data we store

The app stores the following in its database:

  • Your Google account ID.
  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens that let the app act on your YouTube and Spotify accounts, and their expiry times.
  • Playlists you create, add, copy or generate in the app: the playlist name, which platform(s) it is on, its YouTube and Spotify playlist IDs, and its song list.
  • For playlists marked "Keep Synced": a record of which songs were matched between the two platforms at the last sync, change markers from each platform, and any sync errors.
  • For "Generate with AI": the text prompt you typed and how many AI tokens the request used (used to enforce daily usage limits).

The app also keeps a shared cache that maps song titles and artists to Spotify track IDs and YouTube video IDs. This cache is not linked to any user.

The app uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses and request times) for security and troubleshooting.

4. How we use your data

We use your data only to provide the features you use in the app: signing you in, showing your playlists and their songs, creating, copying, syncing and deleting playlists, automatic syncing of "Keep Synced" playlists, and generating playlists with AI.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or marketing, use it to build profiles of you, or allow humans to read your YouTube or Spotify data, except where needed for security, to comply with the law, or with your explicit permission (for example, if you ask us to help debug a problem).

5. Google API Services – Limited Use

Connect to the Music's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. YouTube API Services

Connect to the Music uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app:

  • Google (YouTube) and Spotify: to read and update your playlists when you use the app.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): when you use "Generate with AI," the text prompt you typed is sent to Anthropic so it can suggest songs. No YouTube or Spotify account data is sent to Anthropic.
  • Render (app hosting) and Neon (database hosting).
  • cron-job.org: calls the app on a schedule to run automatic syncing. It does not receive any of your data.

We may also disclose data if required by law.

8. How long we keep data

  • Your account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • When you log out, your YouTube and Spotify access tokens are deleted. Refresh tokens are kept so you can sign back in without reconnecting Spotify.
  • When you delete your account, your account, tokens, playlists, sync records and AI prompt records are permanently deleted from our database.

9. Deleting your data and revoking access

  • In the app: open Actions → Delete Account to permanently delete all of your data from our database. This does not delete the playlists themselves from YouTube or Spotify.
  • Google: you can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
  • Spotify: you can remove the app's access from your Spotify account's Manage Apps page.
  • You can also email us at the address below and we will delete your data within 30 days.

10. Security

All traffic to the app uses HTTPS. API keys and secrets are kept on the server and never sent to your browser. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

11. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete their data.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
